Whole-figure sheet for M16 (rig-first pipeline). Unlike `prompt.txt`, this sheet draws
complete characters, head attached to body, and the head/torso split is measured and cut
afterwards by the pipeline rather than prompted for. See `TOONSTRIP_DEV_PLAN.md` §16.

Output at the largest resolution the model supports (4K preferred, 2048px minimum). Each
cell holds a full standing figure, so the head is roughly one seventh of cell height: at
4096px the head lands near 195px against the packer's 256px cell, at 2048px it lands near
97px and the faces will be too soft to use. Resolution is the binding constraint on this
sheet.

---

Draw a 3 columns x 3 rows character sheet, 9 cells, equal cell sizes, thin gutters
between cells, on a plain flat mid-grey background. Print each cell's label in a small
band at the top of that cell. Do not draw guide lines.

Every cell is the same character, drawn at the same size and in the same style
throughout — this is one character sheet, not 9 separate drawings. Flat colour, clean
hard-edged outlines, no shadows, no gradients, no background scenery, no ground shadow.
Every cell: 3/4 view, head and body turned to face screen-right, same angle throughout —
not a frontal portrait (the renderer mirrors this art horizontally to face the other way
when two characters share a scene, so it must start off-axis, not symmetric).

Each cell holds ONE complete standing figure: head, neck, shoulders, arms, torso, legs,
down to footwear, feet touching the bottom edge of the cell, not floating above it with
dead space below. The head is attached to the body and the neck is drawn normally — this
is a whole character, not a separated part.

Hold these three things identical in all 9 cells, because the pipeline measures the neck
join from one cell and reuses it for all of them:

1. **Figure scale.** The same character height in every cell, to within a couple of
   percent. Do not zoom in or out between cells.
2. **Head position.** The head occupies the same place in every cell, at the same size
   and the same tilt. Only the eyes, brows and mouth change between cells.
3. **Collar line.** The shoulders and the base of the neck sit at the same height in
   every cell. Arms move; the collar does not.

A raised arm must stay inside the cell with clear margin, not extend past the cell edge
or into the label band.

Cells, in reading order, each named `<emotion>-<pose>`:

Row 1: NEUTRAL-neutral, HAPPY-wave, COY-pointother
Row 2: BORED-shrug, SCARED-pointself, LAUGH-doublepoint
Row 3: SAD-neutral, ANGRY-neutral, SHOUT-neutral

The first half of each name is the facial expression. The second half is what the arms
are doing: `neutral` arms rest at the sides, `wave` one arm raised in greeting,
`pointother` one arm extended pointing away from the body, `pointself` one hand indicating
the character's own chest, `doublepoint` both arms extended pointing outward, `shrug` both
palms turned up at waist height, shoulders raised. The face and the arms are independent:
draw the named expression and the named arm pose together in the same figure.

Character: Bronze Age Greek warrior-king, early-to-mid 30s, lean muscular build, olive
skin, short dark curly beard and matching cropped curly hair, bronze cuirass over a
weathered red-brown tunic, a sea-blue cloak clasped at one shoulder, bare legs, leather
sandals, bold black outlines.
